Does the loss still count this year?

Take a loss, then get back into the same ticker within 30 days — including by assignment on a new put — and the loss is deferred, not deleted. It moves into the new lot’s basis.

Educational tool, not tax or investment advice. These worksheets follow standard US treatment for equity options: written options that expire are short-term regardless of how long they were open; put premium reduces the basis of assigned shares; call premium is added to proceeds when shares are called away. They do not handle state tax, the 3.8% net investment income tax, straddle rules, Section 1256 contracts (index options such as SPX are taxed 60/40 — different rules entirely), or unqualified covered calls suspending your holding period. Long-term rates are estimated from your marginal bracket. Confirm anything that matters with a professional before you file.
