Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

The short version

The calculators run entirely inside your browser. The trade figures you type — strikes, premiums, dates, share counts — are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no accounts, no login, and no database of your trades.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect the numbers you enter into any calculator.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not store an email address unless you email us yourself.
  • We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics.

What does leave your browser

  • Hosting. This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser user-agent when it serves the page, as any web server does. See GitHub's privacy statement for how they handle it.
  • Fonts. The page loads typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives the request for those font files.

If you email us

If you write to tzehin86@gmail.com, we keep your message and address so we can reply. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share your details.

If we add payments

Should we introduce paid features, card payments would be handled by Stripe, our payment processor. Stripe would receive the details needed to take the payment; we would never see or store your full card number. This page will be updated before any such change takes effect.

Your rights and contact

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to delete it, by emailing tzehin86@gmail.com. Because we do not run accounts, in most cases the answer is that we hold nothing beyond any email you have sent us.
